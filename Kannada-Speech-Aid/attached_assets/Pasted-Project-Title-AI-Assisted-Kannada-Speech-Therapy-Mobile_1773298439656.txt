Project Title:
AI-Assisted Kannada Speech Therapy Mobile Application for Post-Stroke Patients

Project Overview:
Stroke patients often suffer from speech and language disorders such as aphasia or apraxia, making it difficult for them to pronounce words or communicate effectively. While speech therapy sessions help patients recover, many struggle to practice consistently outside clinical environments. Additionally, most existing therapy applications focus mainly on English and lack support for regional languages like Kannada, which many patients in Karnataka prefer.

This project proposes the development of a mobile-based AI-assisted speech therapy application designed specifically for Kannada-speaking stroke patients. The system will provide guided speech exercises, pronunciation training, and AI-based feedback to help patients practice speech rehabilitation independently while allowing therapists to monitor progress.

Problem Statement:
There is a lack of accessible digital speech-therapy tools for Kannada-speaking stroke patients. Patients require an interactive platform that allows them to practice pronunciation and speech exercises in their native language while enabling therapists to track recovery progress.

Objectives:

Develop a mobile application for Kannada speech therapy exercises.

Integrate speech recognition to analyze patient pronunciation.

Provide AI-based feedback to improve speech clarity.

Track therapy progress and performance over time.

Allow doctors or therapists to monitor patient improvement.

Core Features:

Guided Speech Therapy Exercises

Kannada vowels, consonants, words, and simple sentences

Audio pronunciation demonstrations

Repeat-after-me speaking exercises

Speech Recording and Analysis

Microphone input for recording patient speech

Speech-to-text conversion using speech recognition models

Comparison between expected and spoken words

AI-Based Feedback System

Pronunciation accuracy evaluation

Suggestions for improving speech clarity

Adaptive difficulty based on patient performance

Progress Tracking

Record therapy sessions and practice history

Display pronunciation scores and improvement graphs

Weekly or monthly progress reports

Doctor/Therapist Monitoring Dashboard

View patient activity and progress

Analyze therapy effectiveness

Provide feedback or modify therapy exercises

Accessibility

Simple interface suitable for elderly stroke patients

Kannada language interface and voice guidance

Technology Stack (Proposed):

Mobile Development: Flutter or React Native

Backend: Python (Flask or FastAPI)

AI Tools: Speech recognition models such as Whisper or Google Speech-to-Text

Database: Firebase or MongoDB

Data Visualization: Dashboard for patient progress tracking

Feasibility:
The project is feasible for a student team because it utilizes existing speech recognition APIs and mobile development frameworks. The system will initially support a limited set of Kannada therapy words and exercises, allowing the team to develop a functional prototype within the academic project timeline.

Expected Outcome:
The final system will be a working prototype mobile application that helps stroke patients practice Kannada speech therapy exercises, receive AI-based pronunciation feedback, and track their recovery progress. The platform will also assist speech therapists in monitoring patient rehabilitation outside clinical sessions.

Applications:

Stroke rehabilitation centers

Speech therapy clinics

Home-based therapy practice

Assistive technology for speech-impaired individuals
